      Teen Patti

      Menu

      Leaving gives up your seat and your chips. Logging out or closing the tab doesn't — log in again from any device to get back to your seat.

      How it plays

      Boot: everyone in the hand puts in the boot, then gets 3 cards face down.

      Blind / Seen: you can play without looking (blind) or look at your cards (seen). Blind pays the current stake; seen pays double.

      Chaal keeps the stake. Raise doubles it (up to 32× the boot). Pack folds.

      Show: with two players left, either one can pay to ask for a show. Both hands are revealed and the better hand wins. If they tie, the player who asked loses.

      Hands, best to worst:

      1. Trail (three of a kind)
      2. Pure sequence (straight flush)
      3. Sequence (straight)
      4. Color (flush)
      5. Pair
      6. High card

      Sequences: A-K-Q is highest, then A-2-3, down to 4-3-2. On your turn you have to act — if you disconnect during it, you're packed after 30 seconds.
